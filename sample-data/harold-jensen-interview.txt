Research interview: Harold Jensen, Course Superintendent, Pine Valley Golf Club
Interviewer: Ian

Ian: Tell me about your role and what you look after day to day.

Ian: What does that cost you?

Ian: What have you tried to make it easier?

Harold: I'd get my crew out of that ravine for good. But honestly, the board wants all the grounds work kept in-house for now, so I'm not shopping for outside help.
